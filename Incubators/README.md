# India Incubators Master Directory

This directory contains the verified, deduplicated database of **1,068 Incubators** operating across India.

## Definition (Incubators)
Long-term support hubs (typically **12 to 24+ months**) focused on zero-to-one execution—transforming ideas into working prototypes. Hosted primarily by academic institutions (IITs, IIMs, NITs, Central Universities) or government missions, they offer physical lab/office space, advanced R&D equipment, and non-dilutive grants.

## Statutory Authority Schemes Covered
1. **Startup India Seed Fund Scheme (SISFS - DPIIT):** 178+ approved incubators authorized to disburse ₹945 Cr seed capital (₹3 Cr to ₹5 Cr grant per incubator).
2. **NITI Aayog Atal Innovation Mission (AIM):** 93+ Atal Incubation Centres (AICs) & ACICs established with up to ₹10 Cr grant-in-aid.
3. **MeitY Startup Hub (MSH):** 578+ TIDE 2.0 and GENESIS ICT, deep-tech, and electronics incubation centres.
4. **DST – NIDHI (Dept. of Science & Technology):** 101+ Technology Business Incubators (TBIs), inclusive-TBIs (i-TBIs), NIDHI-SSP Seed Support Program nodes, and flagship Centres of Excellence (up to ₹50 Cr).
5. **BIRAC – BioNEST (Dept. of Biotechnology):** 69+ bio-incubators supporting medical devices, healthcare, life sciences, and agri-bio startups.

## Files
- `india_incubators_master.xlsx` — Excel formatted master file.
- `india_incubators_master.csv` — UTF-8 CSV data export.

## Schema
1. `name`: Incubator Name / Host Institution
2. `category`: TBI, AIC, BioNEST, i-TBI, Seed Fund Incubator, CoE
3. `type`: Central Govt / DPIIT, NITI Aayog, MeitY, DST, BIRAC, Academic
4. `city`: City / District
5. `state`: State / UT
6. `location`: Full location address
7. `sector_or_stage`: Focus sectors and stage
8. `description`: Core capabilities, labs, and support
9. `website`: Official portal URL
10. `email`: SPOC / contact email
11. `funding_allocation`: Scheme grant or seed corpus support
12. `source_authority`: Governing central authority
13. `source_url`: Reference registry link
